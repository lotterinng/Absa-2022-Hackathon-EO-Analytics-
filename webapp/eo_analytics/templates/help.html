Getting started

  1. Add your field. Open My Fields, zoom to your farm on the satellite map, choose the pentagon tool and click around the edge of the field. Give it a name, crop, planting date and the yield you normally expect.
  2. My Farm shows the latest cloud-free satellite picture of the field:
    • Crop Health (NDVI) – red is weak growth, green/blue is strong.
    • Soil Moisture (NDMI) – red is dry, blue is moist.
    • Crop Yield – how close each field is to the yield you expect.
  3. Statistics shows the last 3 months as charts, with rainfall and modelled soil moisture. You can download the data as a CSV file.
  4. Weather shows today and the next 7 days at your field, plus a simple farm tip.
  5. Agric News has articles from EO Analytics and agricultural news feeds.

Good to know

  • Images come from the European Sentinel-2 satellites, which pass about every 5 days. Clouds hide the ground, so in the rainy season there can be longer gaps.
  • Each pixel is 10 × 10 m, so very small plots (under about 0.1 ha) cannot be measured reliably.
  • The first time you open a field it can take up to a minute to download its images. After that it is fast.
  • Yield figures are estimates for planning, not guarantees.
